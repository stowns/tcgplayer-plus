# Release notes

## 1.4.0

### Add cards from search results
- Every card on a TCGplayer search or set page now has an **Add to watch list** button, so you no longer need to open each product page.
- If the card comes in more than one printing, such as Normal and Reverse Holofoil, you choose the printing first. Each printing shows how many listings it has. A card with only one printing goes straight to your watch lists.

### Several printings of one card
- A watch list can now hold the same card in more than one printing. Each printing is its own entry, with its own Ask, price trend and price target.
- The printing appears after the rarity, for example "SV: 151 · 007/165 · Common · Reverse Holofoil".

### More reliable Ask changes
- The arrow next to the Ask no longer shows huge jumps such as "+663%" caused by a listing that briefly appeared at a mistaken price.
- An earlier Ask is now compared against the card's cheapest sale of the last month. An Ask under half that price is treated as a mistake and ignored.
